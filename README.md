## 📌 Project Overview

**Tree Plantation Calculator** is a sustainability-focused web platform designed to help individuals, students, organizations, communities, and environmental planners understand the environmental impact of tree plantation.

The platform goes beyond a basic tree calculator.

It provides tools for:

* 🌳 Tree plantation planning
* 🌍 CO₂ reduction estimation
* 💨 Oxygen production estimation
* 💧 Water requirement analysis
* 💰 Plantation cost estimation
* 🌱 Tree species recommendation
* 🌦️ Climate-based recommendations
* 🪨 Soil suitability analysis
* 📅 Plantation scheduling
* 📊 Long-term environmental impact analysis
* 🧮 Carbon-offset planning
* 📄 Ecological report generation
* 📈 Interactive sustainability analysis

The primary objective is to transform a simple question:

> **"How many trees should I plant?"**

into a more meaningful sustainability decision:

> **"Which trees should I plant, where should I plant them, how many are required, what will they cost, how much water will they need, and what environmental impact can they create over time?"**

# 🚨 Problem Statement

Tree plantation campaigns often focus on the number of trees planted rather than the effectiveness of the plantation.

Several important questions are usually left unanswered:

* How many trees are actually required?
* Which species are suitable for the location?
* How much CO₂ can the trees absorb?
* What is the expected oxygen contribution?
* How much water will the plantation require?
* What will the estimated plantation cost be?
* Is the selected species suitable for the soil?
* Is the climate suitable for the selected species?
* When is the best time to plant?
* What could the long-term environmental impact be?

The **Tree Plantation Calculator** addresses these questions through a centralized interactive platform.

## 🌍 2. Carbon Footprint Offset Planner

Users can enter their annual CO₂ footprint and select a tree species.


```text

 │        │
```

# 📊 Calculation Model

The basic carbon-offset calculation follows the principle:

```text
Required Trees =
Ceiling(
    Annual CO₂ Footprint
    /
    CO₂ Absorption Per Tree Per Year
)
```

For projected absorption:

```text
Projected CO₂ =
Number of Trees
×
Annual CO₂ Absorption
×
Number of Years
```

The advanced optimization module additionally evaluates multiple plantation factors such as:

```text
CO₂ Impact
+
Cost
+
Water Requirement
+
Tree Characteristics
=
Plantation Efficiency
```

> **Important:** Environmental calculations are estimates based on the project's underlying tree/environmental data. Actual carbon sequestration varies by species, age, climate, soil, health, location, management practices, and measurement methodology.
