Know your air

CO₂, AQI & PM explained with friendly visuals

What is CO₂?

Carbon dioxide traps heat. Our calculators estimate how many trees you need to absorb tonnes of CO₂ over 5, 10, and 20 years.

Tip: Fast growers like Bamboo and Neem soak up CO₂ quickly.

Why AQI matters

Air Quality Index combines PM, NO₂, and ozone. We classify cities into five AQI bands and match species that thrive in each band.

Live AQI pulls help prioritize most polluted neighborhoods.

What is PM?

PM2.5 and PM10 are microscopic particles. Dense, leafy canopies filter these particles and cool the ground at the same time.

Layer species with overlapping canopies for maximum filtration.
